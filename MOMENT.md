# The moment

The people below are **fictional**. They exist to make the rules concrete, and every place they appear says so.

**Lena Hoffmann (fictional), 31, night-shift pharmacist in Leipzig, Germany.** Her best friend from university,
**Arjun Mehta (fictional), 27, a product designer, moved back to Pune, India.** They promised to keep in touch. For a
month they did. Then the messages got shorter, then weekly, then birthdays only.

## The moment, with the clock

Lena picks Chog #1462 (token 1; it looks left, wearing BTC glasses). She sends Arjun a link. He picks Chog #338 (token
846; it looks right) and joins. The Chog Twain day closes at 00:00 UTC: 02:00 in Leipzig (CEST), 05:30 in Pune. Lena taps
"I'm here" after her shift at 22:40. Arjun taps with his first chai at 07:10. Whoever arrives second sees both Chogs step
closer.

On day four Arjun forgets. The Chogs drift apart and a slice of his pledge moves to Lena. That night he sends her a voice
note. On day nine they meet. Both get a postcard. Both Chogs remember. They start a 30-day walk.

## The third person

Lena's sister has no Chog and no pledge. She lights a lantern for them with $CHOG. The night Arjun's flight is delayed,
the lantern keeps the day.

## The holder

Mei Tanaka (fictional) in Osaka owns Chog #338. She never knew. One morning she sees on its Friend Book that her Chog has
met eleven friends this month, walked by people she has never met.

## Founder stake

Kinnoski (@0xkinno) is a Web3 content creator who has seen engagement campaigns farmed. He walks the first real pairs
