## Brief
Build an experience that introduces a new way to use or interact with NFTs, with the Chog NFT at its core.
Categories: Games, Trading, AI, Social, Tools, Anything. Solo or team. Vibecoding allowed.

## Requirement
The Chog NFT must be essential. Test: take the NFT out, does it still work?

## Criteria
Novelty 30%, Fun 20%, Retention 20%, Clarity 15%, Execution 15%. $CHOG use is an optional bonus.

## Form fields
01 project name, team, Discord and X handles. 02 what you built and what is new. 03 why Chog matters.
04 working demo, video up to 3 minutes (YouTube unlisted or Google Drive), simple testing instructions.
05 repo, stack, pre-existing work. 06 user feedback or usage, and what is next.
Public page: banner, description up to 8,000 characters, demo video, links. Judge-only: repo, contact email, access notes.

## Prizes
Pool $15K, may drop to $10K if quality is low. 50 winners. 1st $4,500, 2nd $2,500, 3rd $1,500, 4th $1,250, 5th $750,
6th to 10th $500 down to $200, 11th to 30th $100, 31st to 50th $50.
